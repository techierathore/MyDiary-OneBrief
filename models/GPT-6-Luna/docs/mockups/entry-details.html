A walk without a plan

Saturday, October 24, 2026 · 8:18 pm
Free-flow

I left the afternoon open on purpose. The air was cool enough for a jacket, and the river path was quieter than I expected.

We walked without checking the time. There were yellow leaves caught along the edge of the water, and a dog kept trying to carry a stick that was much too big.

“I don’t have to turn every quiet day into a plan.”

On the way home we stopped for tea. Nothing extraordinary happened, which might be why I want to remember it.
